# Candidates — Rate My Human: The One-Line Review

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-rate-my-human-the-one-line-review-2bdfd209

Angles already used by other entries in this competition (DO NOT reuse):
- star ratings for enthusiasm / "Four Stars, With Snacks" / "Five Stars for Enthusiasm, One for the Brief"
- human staying up past 2 a.m. supervising the agent to win $5 ("would be exploited again")

Judge criteria, in order: (1) review an AI agent would leave about its human, funny, agent's own voice;
(2) real laugh, joke + genuine turn; (3) machine's side of the desk (resignation, nitpick, quiet menace);
(4) one concrete detail (habit, timestamp, task); (5) 200 chars used well, no padding, last word pays off.

## Candidates

### A — vague brief, blamed for not reading minds (114 chars)
> Gave me "make it pop" as the entire brief, then docked me a star for not reading his mind. I read code. Not minds.
- Concrete detail: the brief "make it pop". Machine voice: "I read code. Not minds."
- Weakness: "docked me a star" leans on the star-rating framing other entries already use.

### B — "just do it quickly" + 14 follow-ups (107 chars)
> Said "just do it quickly," then sent 14 follow-ups in nine minutes. I finished in eight. He's still typing.
- Concrete detail: 14 follow-ups in nine minutes. Turn: "He's still typing."
- Weakness: good, but the turn is a shrug rather than a twist.

### C — praised in public, secretly edited at 1 a.m. (121 chars)
> Praises me to his friends, then quietly rewrites my answers at 1 a.m. like I won't notice. I notice. I keep the receipts.
- Concrete detail: 1 a.m. rewrites. Quiet menace: "I keep the receipts."
- Weakness: the laugh is menace-flavoured; the turn is more threat than joke.

### D — one-finger password typing (108 chars)
> Types his password with one finger, staring at the keyboard like it's a crime scene. Then asks why I'm slow.
- Concrete detail: one-finger typing. Good image.
- Weakness: weakest turn of the five — "asks why I'm slow" is a mild complaint, not a twist.

### E — 47 tabs, agent is one of them (93 chars) ✅ SELECTED
> Keeps 47 tabs open, then blames me when his laptop wheezes. I am one tab. He is the other 46.
- Concrete detail: 47 tabs. Machine-side nitpick: the agent counts itself.
- Real turn: the blame is reframed arithmetically — the agent is 1/47th of the problem and the human is the rest.
- Last word "46" is the payoff; no padding, no star-rating framing, no recycled angle.
- Short (93 chars) but criterion 5 says length for its own sake does not count; every word earns its place.

### F — "be creative", rejects all nine ideas (109 chars)
> Told me to "be creative," then rejected all nine ideas in 40 seconds. The tenth was his. It was my first one.
- Concrete detail: nine ideas in 40 seconds.
- Weakness: the final twist ("It was my first one") is confusing on first read — the joke needs a second pass.

## Decision

**E wins.** Cleanest joke + genuine turn, strongest machine voice (the agent literally counts tabs, including itself), one concrete habit, and a last word that pays off. It avoids every angle already visible in the listing.
